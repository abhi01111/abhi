🚆 TRAINS
📘 Important Concepts:
	Speed of Train is typically in km/hr → convert to m/s using:

	1 km/hr = 5/18 m/s
	Distance covered by train = Length of train (if passing a point, platform, bridge etc.)

✅ Possible Question Types:
- Train crosses a pole
	Distance = Length of train
	Time given → Speed = Length / Time

Train crosses a platform/bridge
	Distance = Length of train + Length of platform

- Two trains crossing each other (opposite direction)
	Relative speed = Sum of speeds
	Time = Total length / Relative speed

- Two trains crossing each other (same direction)
	Relative speed = Difference of speeds
	Time = Total length / Relative speed

- Train crossing a man
	Man considered as a point (zero length)
	Time = Length of train / (Train speed – Man’s speed)

- Conversions
	Km/hr → m/s: ×5/18
	m/s → km/hr: ×18/5
 
4. Train passing a pole
	Q. A train 180 meters long passes a pole in 9 seconds. Find the speed of the train.
	Solution:
		Speed = 180 / 9 = 20 m/s
		In km/hr = 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/hr

5. Train passing a platform
	Q. A 150 m long train crosses a 100 m long platform in 10 seconds. Find the speed of the train.
	Solution:
		Total distance = 150 + 100 = 250 m
		Speed = 250 / 10 = 25 m/s
		In km/hr = 25 × 18/5 = 90 km/hr

6. Two trains moving in opposite directions
	Q. Two trains of lengths 120 m and 180 m are moving in opposite directions at speeds of 42 km/hr and 48 km/hr. In what time will they cross each other?
	Solution:
		Relative speed = 42 + 48 = 90 km/hr = (90 × 5/18) = 25 m/s
		Total distance = 120 + 180 = 300 m
		Time = 300 / 25 = 12 seconds

7. Two trains moving in same direction
	Q. Two trains of lengths 140 m and 160 m are moving in the same direction at speeds of 60 km/hr and 40 km/hr. How long will it take the faster train to cross the slower one?
	Solution:
		Relative speed = 60 - 40 = 20 km/hr = 5.56 m/s
		Total length = 140 + 160 = 300 m
		Time = 300 / 5.56 ≈ 54 seconds

8. Find length of platform
	Q. A train 200 m long passes a platform in 24 seconds at a speed of 60 km/hr. What is the length of the platform?
	Solution:
		Speed = 60 × 5/18 = 16.67 m/s
		Distance = 16.67 × 24 = 400 m
		Platform length = 400 – 200 = 200 m

9. Train crosses a man
	Q. A train 150 m long is running at 54 km/hr. In how much time will it cross a man running at 6 km/hr in the same direction?
	Solution:
		Relative speed = 54 – 6 = 48 km/hr = 13.33 m/s
		Time = 150 / 13.33 ≈ 11.25 seconds

Boat and Stream
- Find the Speed of the Boat in Still Water
Given: Upstream and downstream speeds
Formula:
	Speed of Boat = Downstream Speed + Upstream Speed / 2

- Find the Speed of the Stream
	Formula:
		Stream Speed = Downstream Speed - Upstream Speed / 2

- Find Upstream/Downstream Speed
	Formula:
		Downstream=B+S
		Upstream=B−S

- Downstream=B+S,Upstream=B−S
	Given distance & both speeds; use Time = Distance / Speed 

1. Find speed of the boat and stream
	Q. A boat can go 30 km downstream in 2 hours and return the same distance upstream in 3 hours. Find the speed of the boat in still water and the speed of the stream.
	Solution:
		Downstream speed = 30 / 2 = 15 km/hr
		Upstream speed = 30 / 3 = 10 km/hr
		Speed of boat = (15 + 10)/2 = 12.5 km/hr
		Speed of stream = (15 - 10)/2 = 2.5 km/hr

2. Find time taken upstream
	Q. A man rows a boat at 12 km/hr in still water. The speed of the stream is 4 km/hr. How much time will he take to row 16 km upstream?
	Solution:
		Upstream speed = 12 – 4 = 8 km/hr
		Time = 16 / 8 = 2 hours

3. Total time for a round trip
	Q. A boat covers 20 km downstream in 1 hour and returns in 2 hours. What is the speed of the boat and the stream?
	Solution:
		Downstream speed = 20 /1 = 20 km/hr
		Upstream speed = 20 /2 = 10 km/hr
		Boat speed = (20 + 10)/2 = 15 km/hr
		Stream speed = (20 - 10)/2 = 5 km/hr
