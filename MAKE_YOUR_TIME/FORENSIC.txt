what is mobile cloning and sim-card cloning in details what it dies
	📱 1. Mobile Cloning
		Definition:
			Mobile cloning is the process of copying the identity of one mobile phone to another.
			It duplicates the phone’s unique identifiers (like the IMEI, ESN, or MEID) so the clone behaves like the original.
			
	📶 2. SIM Card Cloning
		Definition:
			SIM card cloning is the process of copying the International Mobile Subscriber Identity (IMSI) and authentication key (Ki) 
			from one SIM card to another. The result is two SIMs with the same identity.
