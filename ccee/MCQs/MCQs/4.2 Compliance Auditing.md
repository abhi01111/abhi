### **Theory Summary for Session 1 & 2: Introduction to Cybersecurity and Auditing**

---

#### **Introduction to Cybersecurity**

- Cybersecurity protects digital systems, networks, and data from unauthorised access, damage, or disruption.
- Core principles:
    - **Confidentiality**: Ensuring data is accessed only by authorised individuals.
    - **Integrity**: Preventing unauthorised alterations to data.
    - **Availability**: Ensuring authorised users have timely access to information.

---

#### **Cybersecurity Challenges for Organisations**

- **Evolving Threat Landscape**: Cybercriminals continuously develop advanced attacks like phishing, ransomware, and APTs.
- **Lack of Skilled Professionals**: Difficulty in hiring and retaining qualified cybersecurity experts.
- **Compliance Pressures**: Increasing regulatory requirements like GDPR and ISO standards.
- **Insider Threats**: Employees or contractors may intentionally or unintentionally compromise security.
- **Budget Constraints**: Organisations may lack adequate resources for robust security measures.

---

#### **Compliance Basics**

- **Definition**: Adherence to laws, regulations, and standards relevant to cybersecurity and organisational operations.
- Examples of compliance standards:
    - GDPR
    - ISO/IEC 27001
    - HIPAA
    - PCI DSS
- Ensures accountability and protection of sensitive information.

---

#### **Types of Security Audits**

- **Compliance Audit**: Verifies adherence to legal or regulatory requirements.
- **Operational Audit**: Assesses internal processes and efficiency.
- **IT Audit**: Evaluates IT systems for security, reliability, and performance.
- **Financial Audit**: Examines financial records and their integrity.

---

#### **Audit Decision Factors**

- **Scope**: Determines which systems or processes are audited.
- **Regulations**: Guides audit requirements (e.g., GDPR, PCI DSS).
- **Risks**: Focuses on areas with higher potential vulnerabilities.
- **Resources**: Ensures adequate budget, tools, and personnel are available.

---

#### **Security Audit Phases**

1. **Planning**:
    - Define audit scope and objectives.
    - Identify critical systems and data.
    - Allocate resources and assign roles.
2. **Execution**:
    - Collect evidence through interviews, system logs, and documentation.
    - Test security controls.
3. **Reporting**:
    - Document findings and provide recommendations.
    - Highlight compliance gaps.
4. **Follow-Up**:
    - Ensure implementation of audit recommendations.

---

#### **Requirements of Internal Audit Team**

- Independence and objectivity to ensure unbiased assessments.
- Proficiency in auditing techniques and cybersecurity standards.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Familiarity with organisational policies and procedures.

---

#### **Principles of Audits**

- **Integrity**: Auditors should act honestly and ethically.
- **Objectivity**: Findings must be based on factual evidence.
- **Confidentiality**: Protect sensitive information obtained during the audit.
- **Independence**: Avoid conflicts of interest.

---

#### **Auditor Personal Abilities**

- Strong communication skills to explain findings effectively.
- Critical thinking for analysing complex systems.
- Detail-oriented approach to identify vulnerabilities.
- Technical expertise in cybersecurity tools and frameworks.
