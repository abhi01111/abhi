#### **Theory Summary: Service Strategy**

1. **General Strategy**: Aligning IT services with organisational goals for value creation.
2. **Competition and Market Space**: Understanding market trends and competitive landscapes.
3. **Service Provider Types**:

    - External: Third-party providers.

    - Financial Management: Budgeting, accounting, and charging.
    - Service Portfolio Management: Strategic oversight of services.
    - Demand Management: Predicting and balancing service demand.
6. **Roles and Responsibilities**: Strategic planners, financial analysts, and portfolio managers.

#### **Theory Summary: Service Design**

    - Comprehensive documentation covering service architecture, SLAs, operational requirements, and acceptance criteria.
3. **Service Catalog Management**:
    
    - Maintains a single source of truth for IT service offerings.
4. **Service Level Management (SLM)**:
    
    - Ensures agreed service levels are met.
    - Includes creation of SLAs and monitoring of service performance.
5. **Capacity Management**:
    
    - Plans for sufficient IT resources to meet current and future demands.
6. **IT Service Continuity**:
    
    - Plans to recover IT services in case of a disaster.
    - Ensures resilience and redundancy.
7. **Information Security**:
    
    - Focuses on data confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

#### **Theory Summary: Service Transition**

1. **Service Asset and Configuration Management (SACM)**:
    
    - Tracks and manages IT assets and configurations to maintain accuracy.
    - Ensures services are built and maintained using authorised configurations.
2. **Transition Planning and Support**:
    
    - Plans and coordinates resources for successful service transitions.
    - Provides schedules, risk assessments, and transition strategies.
3. **Release and Deployment Management**:
    
    - Ensures that changes and updates are tested, validated, and deployed smoothly.
    - Includes creating release packages and validating deployments.
4. **Change Management**:
    
    - Evaluates, authorises, and monitors changes to minimise disruptions.
    - Utilises a Change Advisory Board (CAB) for approvals.
5. **Knowledge Management**:
    
    - Captures and shares information to improve decision-making and service quality.
    - Builds a centralised knowledge base.
6. **Definitive Media Library (DML)**:
    
    - Repository for authorised software versions and documentation.
7. **Key Roles in Service Transition**:
    
    - **Change Manager**: Oversees all aspects of change management.
    - **Transition Manager**: Manages the overall service transition lifecycle.
    - **Release Manager**: Ensures successful deployments of changes.

#### **Theory Summary: Service Operation**

1. **Key Objectives**:
    
    - Maintain stability and reliability of IT services.
    - Manage daily IT operations, incidents, and events effectively.
2. **Event Management**:
    
    - Monitors system-generated events to ensure normal operation.
3. **Incident Management**:
    
    - Focuses on restoring services quickly to minimise business impact.
4. **Problem Management**:
    
    - Identifies and resolves root causes of incidents to prevent recurrence.
    - Can be **reactive** (after incidents) or **proactive** (before incidents).
5. **Service Desk**:
    
    - A single point of contact for users to report incidents or request services.
    - Handles ticketing, escalation, and resolution.
6. **Asset Management**:
    
    - Tracks and maintains IT asset inventory.
    - Ensures optimal usage and lifecycle management of assets.
7. **Balancing Conflicting Goals**:
    
    - Achieves trade-offs between cost efficiency, performance, and reliability.
8. **Technical and Application Management**:
    
    - Provides expertise in infrastructure and application support.
    - Monitors, maintains, and optimises IT systems and applications.

#### **Theory Summary: Continual Service Improvement (CSI)**

1. **Key Objectives**:
    
    - Align IT services with current and future business needs.
    - Identify and implement opportunities for improvement.
2. **Deming Cycle (Plan-Do-Check-Act)**:
    
    - A continuous improvement model used to enhance processes.
3. **7-Step Improvement Process**:
    
    - Steps include defining what to measure, collecting and processing data, and implementing improvements.
4. **Service Improvement Plan (SIP)**:
    
    - A document used to identify, prioritise, and track improvement opportunities.
5. **Metrics and KPIs**:
    
    - **Business Metrics**: Assess how services support business goals.
    - **Service Metrics**: Evaluate service performance.
    - **Process Metrics**: Measure process efficiency and effectiveness.
6. **Post Implementation Review (PIR)**:
    
    - A structured review of completed projects or changes to identify lessons learned.
7. **Benefits of CSI**:
    
    - Improved service quality, alignment with business goals, and increased customer satisfaction.
8. **Critical Success Factors (CSFs)**:
    
    - Clear roles, actionable metrics, and a culture of continuous improvement.
